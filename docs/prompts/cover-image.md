# Cover Image

This page contains the comprehensive image generation prompt used to design the textbook cover and Open Graph social preview image.

!!! prompt "Cover Image Prompt"
    Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
    This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

    **Required specifications:**
    - Format: PNG
    - Wide-landscape format
    - Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
    - This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

    The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color treatment, mascot, and title text.

    **Subject & Tone:**
    Algebra I is a foundational mathematics textbook designed to transition students from arithmetic reasoning to abstract algebraic thinking, introducing variables, equations, functions, and real-world mathematical modeling. The intended audience is junior high and high school students (Grades 8–10) and their educators. The visual tone should be modern, vibrant, approachable, and mathematically precise — inspiring curiosity, pattern recognition, and confidence.

    **Title:**
    Place **Algebra I** in the center of the image in a bold, clean, highly legible modern sans-serif font. Use crisp white lettering with a subtle dark drop shadow and a soft dark gradient scrim directly behind it to guarantee effortless readability against the surrounding mathematical montage. Keep the title prominent and uncluttered.

    **Background Montage:**
    Arrange a balanced montage of the following 8 core algebraic concepts arranged in an orbital composition around the central title, rendered in a consistent polished flat-vector illustration style:
    1. Cartesian Coordinate Plane: A clean 2D grid with glowing x and y axes, marked origin (0,0), and distinct plotted coordinate points in the four quadrants.
    2. Linear Function & Slope: A sharp linear graph line cutting across a grid with a visible slope triangle highlighting "rise over run" (Δy / Δx) and y = mx + b.
    3. Distributive Property Area Model: A divided geometric rectangle diagram visually expanding algebraic factors like (x + 3)(x + 2) into colored partitioned sub-areas (x², 3x, 2x, 6).
    4. Quadratic Parabola: A graceful, smooth U-shaped parabolic curve opening upward on a grid with a highlighted vertex point and symmetrical roots.
    5. Number Systems Hierarchy: Elegant nested concentric rings or Venn diagrams showing the containment of Natural numbers ⊂ Integers ⊂ Rational numbers ⊂ Real numbers.
    6. Exponential Growth Curve: A dynamic curve sweeping steeply upward to model exponential growth and compounding phenomena.
    7. System of Linear Equations: Two distinct intersecting lines of contrasting colors crossing at a single glowing point of intersection (x, y).
    8. Algebraic Balance Scale: A minimalist, stylized balance scale illustrating equation equivalence with variable boxes x and unit counters balanced on opposite pans.

    **Mascot:**
    Place the textbook's pedagogical mascot, **Sage the Fox**, in the lower-left corner of the cover, scaled proportionally so it does not overlap the title or crowd the center:
    - Character: Sage is an intelligent, friendly young orange fox (hex #F57C00) with cream-colored muzzle, chest, and inner ears, and dark brown paws.
    - Accessories: Small round navy-blue spectacles and a cozy royal-blue scarf (hex #1976D2).
    - Pose & Expression: Friendly, inviting smile with warm brown eyes, holding one paw up in a welcoming greeting gesture embodying their motto: "Let's figure this out!"
    - Art Style: Clean modern vector cartoon character matching the overall vector illustration aesthetic.

    **Style & Composition:**
    - Illustration style: Polished modern vector art with clean lines, smooth gradients, and geometric clarity.
    - Color palette: Dominant deep navy and royal blue (#0D47A1, #1976D2) backdrop, vibrant warm orange accents (#F57C00) echoing the mascot, and bright cyan (#00E5FF) and clean white highlights for mathematical curves and axes.
    - Lighting & Mood: Intellectual, optimistic, engaging, and high-energy STEM educational aesthetic.
    - Composition: Symmetrical wide-landscape (1.91:1); title centered with ample negative space; 8 montage elements framing the title in a cohesive orbit; mascot anchored in the lower-left foreground.

    **Avoid:**
    - Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text, garbled symbols, or fake handwriting anywhere in the image.
    - Avoid generic stock-photo clichés (handshakes, isolated glowing lightbulbs, business people pointing at whiteboards).
    - Avoid photorealistic human figures.
    - Do not allow any montage curves, grid lines, or shapes to collide with or obscure the main "Algebra I" title.
