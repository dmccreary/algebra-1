# References: Factoring Polynomials

1. [Factorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorization) - Wikipedia - Broad overview of factoring in algebra, covering polynomial factorization, common techniques, and the relationship between factored and expanded forms of expressions.

2. [Difference of two squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_of_two_squares) - Wikipedia - Explains the identity $a^2 - b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)$ with proofs, geometric interpretations, and examples, directly supporting the chapter's difference-of-squares factoring pattern.

3. [Quadratic equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_equation) - Wikipedia - Covers methods for solving quadratic equations, including factoring, and explains the zero-product property used to find roots once an equation is factored.

4. Algebra 1 (Common Core Edition) - Randall I. Charles - Prentice Hall/Pearson - Chapters on factoring polynomials present GCF, trinomial factoring, special products, and grouping with graduated practice sets matching this chapter's sequence.

5. Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1 - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell - Big Ideas Learning - Provides a full unit on solving quadratic equations by factoring, including the zero product property and factoring completely, with guided examples.

6. [Special Factoring: Differences of Squares](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/specfact.htm) - Purplemath - Step-by-step lesson on recognizing and factoring difference-of-squares expressions, with multiple worked examples that reinforce this chapter's special patterns section.

7. [Factoring](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/factoring.html) - Math Is Fun - Introduces factoring as reverse multiplication, covering common factors, special identities like difference of squares and perfect squares, and multi-step factoring.

8. [Zero Product Property](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/zero-product-property.html) - Math Is Fun - Clear explanation of the zero product property with worked examples solving equations like $(x-5)(x-3)=0$, directly matching this chapter's equation-solving section.

9. [Factoring Polynomials](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/factoring-polynomials/) - GeeksforGeeks - Comprehensive reference covering common-factor extraction, grouping, splitting the middle term for trinomials, and worked practice problems reinforcing multiple techniques from this chapter.

10. [Solving Quadratic Equations by Factoring](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/solvquad.htm) - Purplemath - Details the four-step factoring method for solving quadratics and explains the zero-factor property, connecting factoring skills to equation solving as covered in this chapter.
