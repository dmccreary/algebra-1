# References: Solving Linear Equations

1. [Linear equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_equation) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of linear equations, including the one-variable form $ax + b = 0$ and its solution, providing the formal mathematical foundation for the equation-solving techniques taught in this chapter.

2. [Equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equation) - Wikipedia - Explains what it means to solve an equation, the concept of a solution set, and the distinction between conditional equations and identities, directly supporting the chapter's discussion of solution types.

3. [Elementary algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_algebra) - Wikipedia - Covers the general algebraic strategy of adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing both sides of an equation to isolate a variable, the core technique behind one-step, two-step, and multi-step equations.

4. Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1 - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell - Big Ideas Learning - Chapters on solving linear equations present one-step, two-step, and multi-step equations alongside literal equations, with worked examples that mirror this chapter's progression.

5. Algebra 1 - Carter, Cuevas, Day, Malloy, and Kennedy - Glencoe/McGraw-Hill Education - Provides detailed instruction on solving equations with variables on both sides, equations with fractions and decimals, and rearranging formulas for a specified variable.

6. [Solving One-Variable Linear Equations](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/solvelin.htm) - Purplemath - Step-by-step lesson on isolating a variable using inverse operations in simple addition and subtraction equations, reinforcing the one-step equation strategies introduced early in this chapter.

7. [Solving Literal Equations](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/solvelit.htm) - Purplemath - Worked examples solving formulas such as area, distance, and perimeter for a specified variable, directly matching the chapter's section on literal equations and formula manipulation.

8. [Solving Equations](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/equations-solving.html) - Math is Fun - Explains the "golden rule" of performing the same operation on both sides of an equation, covering combining like terms, clearing fractions, and checking solutions across multiple equation types.

9. [Solve Equations with Variables and Constants on Both Sides](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/8-3-solve-equations-with-variables-and-constants-on-both-sides) - OpenStax Prealgebra 2e - Free textbook section with a general strategy and worked examples for collecting variable terms on one side and constants on the other, matching the chapter's variables-on-both-sides content.

10. [Linear Equations in One Variable](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/linear-equations-in-one-variable/) - GeeksforGeeks - Outlines a structured method for solving linear equations, including equations with fractions and multi-step examples, complementing the chapter's coverage of equations with fractions and multi-step problems.
