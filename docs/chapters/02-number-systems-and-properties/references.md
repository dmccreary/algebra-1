# References: Number Systems and Properties

1. [Real number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_number) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of the real number system, including its construction from rationals and irrationals, decimal representations, and the real number line. Grounds the chapter's discussion of $\mathbb{R}$.

2. [Rational number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_number) - Wikipedia - Detailed explanation of rational numbers as ratios $p/q$ of integers, including terminating and repeating decimal forms. Directly supports the chapter's coverage of fractions and decimal classification.

3. [Commutative property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_property) - Wikipedia - Explains the commutative property of addition and multiplication with formal definitions, historical background, and examples of operations that are not commutative. Reinforces the chapter's properties-of-operations section.

4. Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1 (Common Core Edition) - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell - Big Ideas Learning - Chapter 1 covers real numbers, absolute value, and the properties of operations with guided examples and practice sets that match this chapter's scope.

5. Elementary and Intermediate Algebra: Concepts and Applications - Marvin L. Bittinger, David J. Ellenbogen, and Barbara L. Johnson - Pearson - Chapters on real numbers and properties of real numbers provide rigorous treatment of integers, rationals, and the commutative, associative, and distributive properties.

6. [Properties of Real Numbers](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/properties-of-real-numbers/) - GeeksforGeeks - Tutorial covering commutative, associative, distributive, identity, and inverse properties with worked examples and practice problems. Directly parallels the properties covered in this chapter.

7. [Real Numbers](https://www.mathsisfun.com/numbers/real-numbers.html) - Math Is Fun - Introduces the real number system, distinguishing whole, rational, and irrational numbers and explaining why real numbers exclude imaginary numbers. Supports the chapter's real number classification discussion.

8. [Number Line](https://www.mathsisfun.com/definitions/number-line.html) - Math Is Fun - Concise definition and illustration of the number line as a tool for ordering numbers and visualizing addition and subtraction. Matches the chapter's number line section.

9. [Absolute Value](https://www.mathsisfun.com/numbers/absolute-value.html) - Math Is Fun - Explains absolute value as distance from zero, with notation, examples, and non-negativity rules. Reinforces the chapter's formal definition and examples of $|a|$.

10. [Cartesian Coordinates](https://www.mathsisfun.com/data/cartesian-coordinates.html) - Math Is Fun - Describes the x-axis, y-axis, ordered pairs, and the four quadrants of the coordinate plane, with diagrams showing sign patterns. Supports the chapter's coordinate system section.
