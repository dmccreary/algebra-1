# References: Quadratic Functions and Equations

1. [Quadratic function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_function) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive article covering the properties of quadratic functions, including standard and vertex forms, parabola graphs, vertex, axis of symmetry, and maximum/minimum values discussed throughout this chapter.

2. [Quadratic equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_equation) - Wikipedia - Detailed overview of quadratic equations, including solution methods (factoring, completing the square, the quadratic formula), the discriminant, and the nature of real and complex roots.

3. [Quadratic formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_formula) - Wikipedia - In-depth explanation of the quadratic formula's derivation from completing the square, its use in solving any quadratic equation, and its relationship to the discriminant.

4. Larson, Ron and Laurie Boswell. *Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1*. Big Ideas Learning, 2019. - A widely used Algebra 1 textbook with a full chapter on quadratic functions and equations covering graphing, factoring, completing the square, and the quadratic formula.

5. Charles, Randall I. *Prentice Hall Algebra 1*. Pearson, 2015. - Standard Algebra 1 textbook that presents quadratic functions, parabola graphing, and all major solution methods for quadratic equations with extensive practice problems.

6. [Solving Quadratic Equations by Factoring](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/solvquad.htm) - Purplemath - Step-by-step lesson on solving quadratics by factoring using the zero-product property, with worked examples and common student mistakes, directly supporting the factoring method taught in this chapter.

7. [Completing the Square](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/completing-square.html) - Math Is Fun - Clear walkthrough of the completing-the-square process, transforming ax² + bx + c into a(x + d)² + e form, matching the step-by-step method presented in this chapter.

8. [The Quadratic Formula](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/quadratic-equation.html) - Math Is Fun - Explains the quadratic formula, standard form, and the discriminant's role in determining real, repeated, or complex solutions, reinforcing the formula and discriminant sections of this chapter.

9. [Axis of Symmetry of a Parabola](https://www.mathwords.com/a/axis_symmetry_parabola.htm) - Mathwords - Reference page defining the axis of symmetry formula x = -b/(2a) with worked examples from both standard and vertex form, supporting the graphing and vertex sections of this chapter.

10. [Imaginary Numbers](https://www.mathsisfun.com/numbers/imaginary-numbers.html) - Math Is Fun - Introduction to the imaginary unit i and complex numbers, showing how they arise when solving quadratics with negative discriminants, connecting to the complex number section of this chapter.
