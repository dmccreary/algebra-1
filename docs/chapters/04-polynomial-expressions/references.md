# References: Polynomial Expressions

1. [Polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of polynomial expressions, including degree, standard form, terminology, and the algebra of addition, subtraction, and multiplication that anchors this chapter.

2. [FOIL Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FOIL_method) - Wikipedia - Explains the First-Outer-Inner-Last mnemonic for multiplying two binomials, with worked examples that mirror the chapter's binomial multiplication section.

3. [Difference of Two Squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_of_two_squares) - Wikipedia - Describes the $a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)$ identity and its algebraic proof, directly supporting the chapter's special products coverage.

4. *Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1* (Student Edition), Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell, Big Ideas Learning, 2019 - Print textbook with a full chapter on polynomial equations and factoring, including standard form, degree, and special product patterns aligned to this chapter's scope.

5. *Glencoe Algebra 1* (Common Core Edition), McGraw-Hill Education, 2018 - Widely used Algebra 1 textbook with dedicated lessons on adding, subtracting, and multiplying polynomials and on special products like difference of squares and perfect square trinomials.

6. [Adding and Subtracting Polynomials Review](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra/x2f8bb11595b61c86:polynomial-arithmetic/x2f8bb11595b61c86:add-subtract-polynomials/a/adding-and-subtracting-polynomials-review) - Khan Academy - Practice-oriented review article covering combining like terms to add and subtract polynomials, matching the chapter's horizontal and vertical methods.

7. [Polynomials: Definitions](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/polydefs.htm) - Purplemath - Defines polynomial terminology including terms, coefficients, leading term, degree, and classification by term count and degree, reinforcing the chapter's classification section.

8. [Polynomials](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/polynomials.html) - Math is Fun - Beginner-friendly introduction to what makes an expression a polynomial, degree, and standard form, useful as a gentler on-ramp before the chapter's formal definitions.

9. [Add and Subtract Polynomials](https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/6-1-add-and-subtract-polynomials) - OpenStax Elementary Algebra 2e - Free textbook section covering polynomial classification, degree, and combining like terms to add and subtract, with real-world application examples like the chapter's word problems.

10. [Multiplying Polynomials](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/polynomials-multiplying.html) - Math is Fun - Step-by-step guide to multiplying monomials, binomials, and larger polynomials, including the FOIL method, directly supporting the chapter's multiplication and FOIL sections.
