# References: Systems of Equations and Inequalities

1. [System of linear equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_of_linear_equations) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of linear systems, including geometric interpretation, solution existence, and classification as consistent, inconsistent, or dependent, foundational to this chapter's core topic.

2. [Linear inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_inequality) - Wikipedia - Explains linear inequalities in one and two variables, boundary lines, and half-plane regions, directly supporting the chapter's coverage of systems of inequalities and boundary lines.

3. [Feasible region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feasible_region) - Wikipedia - Describes how overlapping regions from multiple inequalities define a set of feasible solutions, matching the chapter's treatment of solution regions for systems of inequalities.

4. Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1 - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell - Big Ideas Learning - Chapter on systems of linear equations presents graphing, substitution, and elimination side by side with worked examples and real-world application problems used throughout this chapter.

5. Glencoe Algebra 1 (Common Core Edition) - Carter, Cuevas, Day, Malloy, and Holliday - McGraw-Hill Education - Provides a full unit on solving and graphing systems of equations and inequalities, including consistent/inconsistent and independent/dependent classification with practice sets.

6. [Systems of Linear Equations](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/systems-linear-equations.html) - Math is Fun - Introduces systems of equations, then walks through substitution and elimination methods with clear examples, matching the chapter's step-by-step approach to these two algebraic solution methods.

7. [Systems of Linear Equations: Solving by Graphing](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/systlin1.htm) - Purplemath - Demonstrates how a solution corresponds to the intersection point of two graphed lines, verified by substitution, directly supporting the chapter's graphing method section.

8. [Systems of Linear Inequalities: Solving by Graphing](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/syslneq.htm) - Purplemath - Walks through graphing multiple inequalities on one plane, using solid versus dashed boundary lines and shading to find the overlapping solution region described in this chapter.

9. [Graphical Solution of Linear Inequalities in Two Variables](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/graphical-solution-of-linear-inequalities-in-two-variables/) - GeeksforGeeks - Explains how a boundary line divides the plane into half-planes and how test points determine the shaded solution side, reinforcing the chapter's boundary line and solution region concepts.

10. [Solving Systems of Equations: Graphing, Substitution, Elimination](https://courses.lumenlearning.com/csn-precalculusv2/chapter/cr-24-solving-systems-of-equations-graphing-substitution-elimination/) - Lumen Learning - Compares all three solution methods and classifies systems as independent, inconsistent, or dependent, mirroring the exact structure and methods taught in this chapter.
