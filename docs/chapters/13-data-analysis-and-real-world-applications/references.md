# References: Data Analysis and Real-World Applications

1. [Scatter Plot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scatter_plot) - Wikipedia - Explains how scatter plots display paired data on a coordinate plane to reveal relationships between two variables, directly supporting this chapter's lessons on creating and reading scatterplots.

2. [Correlation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correlation) - Wikipedia - Covers how correlation measures the strength and direction of association between variables, including the important distinction between correlation and causation emphasized in this chapter.

3. [Simple Linear Regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_linear_regression) - Wikipedia - Describes the statistical method for fitting a straight line to paired data by minimizing squared errors, matching the chapter's coverage of line of best fit and linear regression.

4. *Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1* (Common Core Edition) - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell - Big Ideas Learning - Includes chapters on scatter plots, correlation, lines of best fit, and arithmetic and geometric sequences with practice problems aligned to this chapter's topics.

5. *Glencoe Algebra 1* (Common Core Edition) - Carter, Cuevas, Day, Malloy - McGraw-Hill Education - Provides instructional sections on statistics and data analysis, linear regression, sequences, and exponential growth models used throughout this chapter.

6. [Scatter (XY) Plots](https://www.mathsisfun.com/data/scatter-xy-plots.html) - Math Is Fun - Illustrated tutorial on plotting paired data, recognizing positive, negative, and no correlation, and sketching a line of best fit, mirroring the chapter's scatterplot and correlation sections.

7. [Least Squares Regression](https://www.mathsisfun.com/data/least-squares-regression.html) - Math Is Fun - Step-by-step walkthrough of the least-squares formulas for slope and intercept, the same regression method the chapter uses to calculate lines of best fit from data.

8. [Describing Relationships Between Two Quantitative Variables](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/describing-relationships-quantitative-data) - Khan Academy - Video lessons and practice on scatterplots, correlation strength, and trend lines that reinforce the chapter's data analysis and prediction skills.

9. [Arithmetic Sequences and Sums](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/sequences-sums-arithmetic.html) - Math Is Fun - Explains common difference, the explicit formula for arithmetic sequences, and their connection to linear patterns, supporting the chapter's arithmetic sequence section.

10. [Compound Interest](https://www.mathsisfun.com/money/compound-interest.html) - Math Is Fun - Details the compound interest formula and how repeated percentage growth compounds over time, directly supporting the chapter's compound interest and exponential application examples.
