# References: Exponential Functions

1. [Exponential Growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_growth) - Wikipedia - Explains the mathematical definition of exponential growth, its formula, doubling time, and applications in population, finance, and biology, directly supporting this chapter's growth-factor concept.

2. [Exponential Decay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_decay) - Wikipedia - Covers the mathematics of quantities that decrease by a constant proportion over time, including half-life and mean lifetime, reinforcing the chapter's decay-factor and radioactive-decay examples.

3. [Compound Interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_interest) - Wikipedia - Details the compound interest formula, compounding frequency, and derivations, matching the chapter's coverage of A = P(1 + r)^t and A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) for investments.

4. *Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1* (Common Core Edition) - Ron Larson and Laurie Boswell, Big Ideas Learning - Includes a dedicated chapter on exponential functions covering growth, decay, and comparisons with linear models, closely mirroring this chapter's scope and sequencing.

5. *Glencoe Algebra 1* (Common Core Edition) - Carter, Cuevas, Day, Malloy, McGraw-Hill Education - Presents exponential functions, growth and decay factors, and real-world modeling problems such as depreciation and population growth, aligning with this chapter's applications.

6. [Exponential growth & decay](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra/x2f8bb11595b61c86:exponential-growth-decay) - Khan Academy - Algebra 1 unit with videos and practice problems on constructing, graphing, and interpreting exponential functions of the form f(x) = a·b^x, matching this chapter's core content.

7. [Exponential Functions](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/expofcns.htm) - Purplemath - Tutorial explaining the form f(x) = ab^x, graph behavior, asymptotes, and growth versus decay, useful for reinforcing the chapter's definitions and graphing steps.

8. [Exponential Growth and Decay](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/exponential-growth.html) - Math is Fun - Accessible explanation of exponential growth and decay with worked examples like population doubling and half-life, supporting the chapter's real-world modeling examples.

9. [6.1 Exponential Functions](https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/6-1-exponential-functions) - OpenStax College Algebra 2e - Free textbook section defining exponential functions, growth versus decay, and compound interest formulas, providing a more advanced treatment that extends the chapter's material.

10. [Exponential Functions](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/exponential-functions/) - GeeksforGeeks - Reference article covering the exponential function formula, growth and decay behavior, and applications to population growth and compound interest, complementing the chapter's worked examples.
