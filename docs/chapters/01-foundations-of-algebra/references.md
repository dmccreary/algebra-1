# References: Foundations of Algebra

1. [Order of Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_operations) - Wikipedia - Explains the standardized sequence (PEMDAS/BODMAS) for evaluating mathematical expressions with multiple operations, the core rule this chapter uses to evaluate and simplify expressions correctly.

2. [Algebraic Expression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_expression) - Wikipedia - Defines how variables, constants, coefficients, and operations combine into algebraic expressions, directly supporting the chapter's coverage of terms, expressions, and expression structure.

3. [Distributive Property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributive_property) - Wikipedia - Describes the property $a(b+c) = ab + ac$ that underlies expanding and factoring expressions, the technique used throughout the chapter's expanding-expressions section.

4. Larson, Ron, and Laurie Boswell. *Big Ideas Math: Algebra 1*. Big Ideas Learning, 2019. Print. - Covers algebraic vocabulary, order of operations, evaluating expressions, and simplifying expressions with the guided-practice approach used in this chapter.

5. Carter, John A., Gilbert J. Cuevas, et al. *Glencoe Algebra 1: Common Core Edition*. McGraw-Hill Education, 2012. Print. - A widely used Algebra 1 text with chapters on expressions, order of operations, properties of real numbers, and combining like terms that parallel this chapter's scope.

6. [Commutative, Associative and Distributive Laws](https://www.mathsisfun.com/associative-commutative-distributive.html) - Math is Fun - Clear, example-driven explanation of the distributive law and related number properties, useful for understanding how to expand expressions like $3(x+2)$ as covered in the chapter.

7. [Like Terms](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/like-terms.html) - Math is Fun - Defines like terms as terms sharing identical variable parts and shows worked examples of combining them, matching the chapter's like-terms and combining-like-terms sections.

8. [Order of Operations](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/orderops.htm) - Purplemath - Step-by-step lessons on applying PEMDAS to expressions with parentheses and exponents, reinforcing the chapter's order-of-operations rules and worked examples.

9. [Substitution](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/substitution-definition/) - GeeksforGeeks - Explains substitution as replacing variables with known values to evaluate expressions and check equation solutions, directly supporting the chapter's substitution and evaluating-expressions sections.

10. [Prime Factorization](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/maths/prime-factorization/) - GeeksforGeeks - Describes methods for breaking numbers into prime factors using factor trees, supporting the chapter's prime factorization section and its connection to finding GCFs and factoring.
