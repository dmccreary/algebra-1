## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Deconstruct algebraic expressions into terms, coefficients, variables, exponents, and constant terms.

### Audience and Prerequisites

Designed for Algebra I students in grade 9. Students should have arithmetic fluency and introductory familiarity with algebraic notation.

### Suggested Activity

1. Ask students to make a prediction before interacting with controls or moving parameters.
2. Hover and select distinct parts of generated algebraic expressions to isolate coefficients, variable factors, and constant terms.
3. Compare the visual model directly with the symbolic algebraic expression.
4. Have students summarize their observations in a single sentence.

### Assessment

Given an expression, students list each term and identify its degree, coefficient, and variable base.
