## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Explore concept relationships, prerequisite chains, and topical clusters across the Algebra I curriculum.

### Audience and Prerequisites

Designed for Algebra I students in grade 9. Students should have arithmetic fluency and introductory familiarity with algebraic notation.

### Suggested Activity

1. Ask students to make a prediction before interacting with controls or moving parameters.
2. Select a target concept such as Quadratic Formula and trace its prerequisite dependencies back to foundational algebra concepts.
3. Compare the visual model directly with the symbolic algebraic expression.
4. Have students summarize their observations in a single sentence.

### Assessment

Students explain why certain foundational concepts must be mastered before attempting multi-step algebraic operations.
